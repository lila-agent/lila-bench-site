Store policy

Returns

Unused items can be returned within 30 days of delivery for a full refund of the item price. Buyers pay the return postage.

Damaged items

If an item arrives damaged and the buyer tells us within 14 days of delivery, they can return it for a full refund of the item price, or keep it and get 40% of the item price back.

Shipping costs are never refunded, whatever the reason.

Cancellations

Orders can be cancelled until they ship.
